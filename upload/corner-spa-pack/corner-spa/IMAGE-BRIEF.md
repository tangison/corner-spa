# Image brief for the image-generating AI agent

Create the photographs for the Corner Spa website. The owner's real photos in assets/photos/ are now the primary style target, so match them first. Match the texture and tonality of assets/style-references/ exactly. Every image is a CLOSE-UP. Show less, not more: one subject, lots of quiet space.

## Look and feel
- Tone: matte, warm off-white (#F5F3EE) backgrounds; taupe/sand (#A89076) and charcoal (#2B2726) as the only accents.
- Light: soft, diffused window light from one side; gentle shadows; no flash, no glow.
- Texture: natural stone grain, dried leaf veins, soft towel weave, fine film grain. Slight speckle like the logo's pebbles.
- Depth: shallow depth of field, subject crisp, edges falling soft.
- Composition: macro or tight crop, subject off-centre, 40 to 60 percent empty space for text overlay.
- People: Namibian skin tones, shown only as hands, shoulders, back or cheek. No full faces.
- Context: calm, understated, Namibian warmth (earth tones, desert sand, dry leaves). No tourist or safari imagery.

## Never include
Text, logos, watermarks, orchids or lotus flowers, candles, bamboo, bright colours, plastic, stock-photo smiles, busy backgrounds, full faces, hands with extra fingers.

Sixty minutes of slow, even strokes across the whole body. The classic choice when everything feels tight.

Questions we hear often

How do cancellations work?

Plans change, we understand. Send us a WhatsApp from the same number you booked with and we move or cancel your appointment. There is no fee for changing a booking in good time.

What is a hot stone massage?

Smooth stones are warmed in water and rest along the back while the massage carries on around them. The warmth is gentle and builds slowly. If you would rather stay without the stones, say so when you book and we do the same massage without them.

Can I have a massage during pregnancy?

Yes, with care. Tell us how far along you are when you book, and we adapt the treatment and the room for you. If anything is high risk, we ask you to check with your doctor or midwife first.

How do I pay?

You pay at the spa on the day of your visit, in Namibian dollars, exactly as listed on the price list. If you would like to know the ways to pay before you come, ask us on WhatsApp and we tell you directly.

All prices in Namibian dollars.

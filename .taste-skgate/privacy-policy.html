Privacy

Privacy policy

Short and honest, because there is not much to say. This site is a quiet shopfront for a spa in Windhoek. The responsible party for the spa is Mendozer Investments cc, Windhoek, Namibia.

What the booking form does

The booking form runs entirely on your device. Your name, treatment and preferred day are arranged into a message, and that message is handed to WhatsApp on your own phone or computer. It is not sent to us, not saved in a database, and not stored on this site. If you close the page before sending, nothing remains.

What we collect, and why

When you message us on WhatsApp, we see whatever you send: your name, your phone number, and the details of the booking. When you visit, we may ask you to fill in a short consultation and intake form. That can include health information, such as allergies, skin sensitivities, medication or pregnancy. We collect this for one reason only: to book your appointment and to give you a safe treatment.

How long we keep it, and who sees it

Booking conversations and consultation forms are kept for as long as we need them to serve you well, and then disposed of. Only Corner Spa staff see them. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone outside the spa.

What we never do

  • We do not keep a mailing list, because there is no list.
  • We do not run advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • We do not ask for card details or payment information here.
  • We do not share anything with third parties for marketing.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you what we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Say the word and we do it, subject to the records a business must keep by law.

WhatsApp and phone calls

Once your message reaches WhatsApp, the conversation happens between you and the spa on that platform. WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply to what they handle.

Hours and getting in touch

We answer during opening hours: Monday to Friday 09:00 to 18:00, Saturday 09:00 to 16:00, closed on Sunday. The spa is at 34 Schonlein Street, Windhoek West, Windhoek, Namibia.

Questions about your privacy

Send us a message on WhatsApp, or write to info@mendozer.com for legal and privacy requests.

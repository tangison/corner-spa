The Corner Spa brand

A quiet reference for anyone making a status image, a flyer or a sign for the spa. Two fonts, three colours, one real logo.

Colours

Warm off-white
#F5F3EE
Background

#2B2726
Text, dark mode ground

Sand
#A89076
Accent bar and decoration

One note on the sand accent: at small text sizes on the warm off-white we use a darkened tone of the same sand so the text stays readable. Decoration and the top bar carry the exact colour.

Fonts

Lora for headlines. Montserrat Light for body text. Two families, nothing else.

The logo

The mark is two pebbles in real rock texture. Use the real image files. Never redraw it flat, never tint it, never place an overlay on it.

On light backgrounds use the dark mark. On dark backgrounds use only the light mark. Files live in the brand pack under assets/logos, from favicon size up to print. The same pebbles are the favicon and the app icon, so the brand follows the spa onto the home screen.

Photographs

Photography is quiet and close: stones, water, cotton, leaves, and hands at work. Pictures of people are shown in a warm monochrome, so every photo sits in the same calm palette. No stock smiles, no props that do not belong to the spa.

Voice

Calm, plain Namibian English. N$ prices. No hype words, no exclamation marks, nothing the spa has not said itself. Booking happens on WhatsApp, so invitations to book simply say that.

If you make something with this kit, keep it breathing: plenty of empty space, one accent, and the real logo doing the talking.

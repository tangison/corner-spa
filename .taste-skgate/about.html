About Corner Spa

Corner Spa is a small day spa at 34 Schonlein Street, Windhoek West, Windhoek. Massage, hot stone, foot spa, nails and underarm waxing, in rooms that stay quiet on purpose. Booking happens on WhatsApp, and the price list is the same on the wall and on this site.

The venture

Corner Spa is the first project of Oryx Institute, which runs the day-to-day operations, and a concept by Mendozer Investments cc, the financing partner. Both names appear on the price list, because both stand behind the spa.

Complaints and feedback are handled by the two organisations, on the spa number +264 81 623 4788 or on the feedback form.

Hours and finding us

Mon to Fri 09:00 to 18:00 · Sat 09:00 to 16:00 · Sun by appointment (Windhoek time). The spa is at 34 Schonlein Street, Windhoek West, Windhoek, Namibia, on the Flexona Organizational Services plot. Get directions.

WhatsApp Booking Flow — From Tap to Confirmed Appointment
Six steps across three phases, powered by one free click-to-chat link
PHASE 1On the website — capture intent
1
Visitor taps the floating WhatsApp button
Green button, bottom-right on every screen. Tap opens WhatsApp directly — no forms, no accounts, no waiting for an email reply.

PHASE 2In the chat — qualify and confirm
3
Instant auto-greeting sets expectations
WhatsApp Business away-greeting replies in seconds: opening hours, typical response time, and a link to the treatment menu.
4
Receptionist confirms treatment, date and therapist quick replies
Saved quick replies answer the four most common requests — massage, facial, couples ritual, gift voucher — in two taps.
5
Deposit link sent when required
For longer rituals or peak slots, a payment link secures the booking: card via DPO Pay, or instant PayToday transfer in NAD.
↓
PHASE 3Locked in — confirm and return
6
Confirmation, 24-hour reminder, review request
The chat itself becomes the appointment card: confirmation with directions, a friendly reminder the day before, and a review link 24 hours after the visit.
